The Negro Motorist Green Book

Safe Spaces for Black Travelers in America

The Green Book was published annually from 1936 to 1966, guiding Black Americans to hotels, restaurants, beauty parlors, and other businesses where they would be welcomed during the era of Jim Crow segregation.

Business Types Over Time, 1937–1955

How the mix of listed business categories shifted across the first two decades of the Green Book, the period in which each listing carried a specific category label. From 1957 onward, editorial reorganisations replaced per-listing labels with a single consolidated heading ("Hotels — Motels — Tourist Homes — Restaurants"), and the 1957 and 1961 editions dropped category labels entirely. Those later volumes are excluded here to keep comparisons meaningful. Use the Scope toggle to compare all entries against US-only listings.
