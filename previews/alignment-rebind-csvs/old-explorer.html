About This Explorer

This tool lets you browse structured data extracted from The Negro Motorist Green Book and related editions published by Victor H. Green from 1937 through the mid-1960s. The Green Books were annual travel guides that helped African American travelers find safe, welcoming accommodations, restaurants, and businesses during the era of racial segregation in the United States.

The source material is digitized and held by the New York Public Library Digital Collections. Entries were extracted from digitized page scans using an OCR and named-entity recognition pipeline.

N.B. There may be OCR or entity-extraction errors in this data. Always use the original source pages as the authoritative source. This explorer is meant to provide one means of engaging with the directories more deeply, and has not been reviewed for 100% accuracy.

The Explorer uses the IIIF standard extensively — entry thumbnails are cropped from the source page images via IIIF Image API, and the source-scan link for each entry uses IIIF Content State to open the viewer at the exact page location.

How to use:

  • Use the edition year checkboxes at the top of the sidebar to filter by edition
  • Use the other filters to narrow by state, city, or category
  • Use the search bar to find specific names, addresses, or notes
  • Click any row to see the full entry and a thumbnail of the source scan
  • Use Export CSV to download the current filtered selection

This is an output of the Directory Pipeline, an open-source tool for extracting structured data from digitized historical directories.
