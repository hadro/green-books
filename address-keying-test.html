Address-keying comparison — current vs. proposed

Side-by-side view of how the current name + raw city index (left) vs. the proposed name + normalized city + address-signature resolver (right) group cross-listings. City normalization folds case, punctuation, trailing state text, and parenthetical neighborhoods ("NEW YORK, N.Y. (HARLEM)" → "new york") for keying only — displayed values keep their original form. The right column is what the new keying would treat as one business. A  ⚠ mixed categories  badge on a group means it spans more than one category — a likely sign of over-merge that warrants a closer look.
