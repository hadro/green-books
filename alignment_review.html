How to answer

The purple outline is the box. The crop shows lines above and below it for context, so look at what the outline actually sits on, not what is visible in the picture.

  • Yes — the outline is on this entry’s own line.
  • No — the outline is on a different line, a heading, or a neighbouring entry. This includes the case where you can see the right text in the crop but the outline is not on it — the box is still wrong.
  • Unsure — the scan is illegible, or the entry genuinely spans lines and you cannot tell. Not for “the box is nearly right”: nearly right is No.

An entry that wraps across two printed lines is a real ambiguity. Either line is defensible; answer Yes if the outline is on either of them.

Rows with no box (489 of them) cannot be judged this way — there is nothing to look at. They are filtered out by default. If you switch them on, they are asking a different question: does a line for this entry exist on the page at all?
